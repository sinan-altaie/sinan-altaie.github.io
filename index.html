About

I run cybersecurity for a global electronics distributor with six operating companies and five e-commerce brands. I joined in 2023 as the company's first dedicated security hire and now lead the program end to end: strategy and budget, 24x7 detection and response, incident command, vulnerability and attack-surface management, email and identity security, and the governance, risk, and compliance portfolio across ISO 27001, CMMC 2.0, SOC 2, PCI DSS, and GDPR.

Before security, I spent a decade running mission-critical data systems for the United Nations and USAID in Baghdad, including the UN mission's country-wide human rights database. That is where I learned to keep systems alive under pressure and to brief leadership with numbers they can act on. The path from data engineering to security leadership is unusual, and it is why I am comfortable in both the SOC and the boardroom.

Selected work

A few programs I designed and led. Details are kept at the level I would share with any peer; the specifics belong to my employer.

AI-driven security operations

A governed agentic SOC pipeline

Designed and built an autonomous triage and response loop on our existing detection stack, constrained by a deterministic policy engine: reversible-only actions, blast-radius caps, a circuit breaker, a kill switch, and a tamper-evident audit trail. Autonomy was earned through a live red-team tabletop and a formal promotion gate, then moved to 24x7 cloud operation under change control. I wrote publicly about the governance model.

Compliance

CMMC 2.0 certification and a public Trust Center

Took the company from a gap analysis to certification in seven months, then renewal. Stood up a public Trust Center, selected and implemented the compliance platform, and launched the ISO 27001:2022 ISMS, the PCI DSS 4.0.1 program through its first internal audit, and a GDPR gap assessment with outside counsel.

Incident command

Running the room when it matters

Incident commander for every significant event since 2024: containment decisions, external DFIR engagement, executive reporting inside the first day, and the remediation programs that follow. I also own the parts nobody sees: insurer notifications, breach counsel, and partner communications.

Email and identity

Replacing the email security platform in three months

Ran a competitive selection, contract, multi-tenant SSO, change communications, and an on-date cutover across two Microsoft 365 tenants, then layered behavioral AI detection on top. Took SPF, DKIM, and DMARC to enforcement on every brand domain and drove MFA, Conditional Access, and the move toward passkeys.

People and culture

Building the team and the human-risk program

Hired and developed a five-person team across Phoenix and Amman, created a Cyber Awareness Lead role, and run monthly phishing simulations, mandatory AI security training, and company-wide security all-hands with hundreds of attendees.

Engineering

Telemetry, testing, and the right tools

Full EDR coverage, a next-generation SIEM with identity threat protection, WAF and NDR assessments validated against production traffic, annual external, internal, and wireless penetration testing with tracked remediation, and a vendor portfolio chosen on evidence, including a documented no-buy.
